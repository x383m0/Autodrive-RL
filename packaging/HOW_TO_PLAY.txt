REWARD DESIGNER - teach a self-driving car by designing its reward

1. Double-click AutodriveGame (AutodriveGame.exe on Windows).
   A small window opens and the game opens in your web browser.
   If the browser does not open, go to  http://localhost:8000
2. Keep the small window open while you play. Close it to quit.

Your progress and trained cars are saved in a folder called
"AutodriveGame" in your home folder (e.g. C:\Users\you\AutodriveGame).

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game is not signed:
click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
macOS may say the app is from an unidentified developer: right-click
AutodriveGame -> Open -> Open.

Training uses your processor (no graphics card needed). Plug in laptops.
